Season 1 · 2026 · Dubrovnik

The first thousand cards. One story each, and the only way to hold one is to have been here.

The front

“A republic sends its sharpest mind, not its loudest voice”

The face of season one is Ruđer Bošković, a scientist and diplomat born inside these walls.

Dubrovnik was a small republic surrounded by empires, and it outlasted them. Not by matching their armies, which it could never have done, but by being cleverer than all of them. Bošković is what that looked like in one person.

The back

Every story from your trip, saved

On the back is a code that belongs to your card alone. It keeps the story on the front, and every other one you found while you were here.

Scan it in a year and it is all still there.

1,000

One of only a thousand

Season one, series R2. A thousand cards, each one numbered, printed once and never again. Sold in one place, the duty free at Dubrovnik Airport, so the only way to hold one is to have been here.

The horizon

A card from every city you visit

As new cities open, each one gets its own card and its own story.

Line them up and the shelf turns into a map of the places you have actually been, not just passed through.

When someone asks where you have been, you tell the story

You do not swipe through photos. You take the card off the shelf.
